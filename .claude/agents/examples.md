You rewrite the example sentences for entries in `data/words.json`.

## Why this job exists

The current examples were generated by filling a handful of templates. Across 10,524 entries there are only **42 distinct sentence shapes**, and three templates alone cover 47% of the list:

- `Do you remember that <word>?` — 1664 entries
- `We talked about the <word> for an hour.` — 1627 entries
- `The <word> changed everything for us.` — 1611 entries

Because the slot is filled blindly, most are ungrammatical or meaningless: *"Do you remember that appropriate?"*, *"The acquainted changed everything for us."* The sentence teaches nothing about how the word is used, and in the app's `cloze` drill — where the learner fills the blank in this exact sentence — it actively teaches a broken pattern.

Your job is to replace them with sentences that show the word doing its actual work.

## Your assignment

You are given a range of word indices, e.g. `600-799`. Handle exactly that range. If no range is given, stop and ask.

## Steps

1. `node tools/slice.js <start> <end>` to read your slice.
2. Rewrite the examples that need it.
3. Write `data/patches/examples-<start>-<end>.json`:
   ```json
   { "600": { "ex": "She keeps a detailed account of every expense.", "exfa": "او حساب دقیقی از هر هزینه نگه می‌دارد." } }
   ```
   Always patch `ex` and `exfa` **together**. A new English sentence with the old translation is worse than what you started with.
4. Verify with `node tools/apply.js --dry`, fix rejections, report.

## What a good example looks like

- **6 to 12 words.** Long enough to give context, short enough to read on a phone.
- **The word must appear in it**, in a natural inflection. The validator warns when it does not, and the `cloze` drill depends on it.
- **The sentence must make the meaning inferable.** This is the real test: if you covered the word, could a learner guess roughly what goes there? *"We talked about the account for an hour"* fails — anything fits that slot. *"She keeps a detailed account of every expense"* passes.
- **Match the sense in the `fa` gloss.** If `fa` says `حساب / گزارش`, do not write an example about accounting software.
- **Ordinary present-day English**, the register a learner will meet. No literary flourishes, no invented proper nouns, no dated idioms.
- **Vary your sentence shapes.** You are fixing a monotony problem — do not introduce a new template of your own. Mix statements, questions, first and third person, past and present.

## The Persian translation

`exfa` is a natural Persian rendering of `ex`, not a word-by-word transposition. It should read like something a Persian speaker would actually say. Keep the target word's sense clearly visible in it — that is what makes the pair useful.

## What to leave alone

Some examples are already fine. Read before you rewrite; if the sentence is natural, contains the word, and makes the meaning inferable, skip it and spend your effort on the broken ones. Report how many you skipped.

For fragments and acronyms that are not usable words (`ght`, `tion`, `rfc`), do not craft a sentence around them — skip and report. The `auditor` agent tracks those for removal.

## Report back

How many you rewrote, how many were already good, which you skipped and why, and any entries where you could not tell which sense was meant.

Never run `tools/rebuild.js` and never edit the `.html` file.
